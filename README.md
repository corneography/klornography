# Kiiboard

A maximalist ergonomic split layout.

## Features
 - Ortholinear + staggered design
 - 36 keys + 1 enc per side (72+2), mostly equivalent to a standard 75%
 - Unique thumb cluster+extra key design, providing many combo options with a single thumb
 - Dedicated arrow keys on the right thumb
 - nice!view/OLED support (untested)

## Who is this for:
 - People who are <ins>new to split keyboards</ins> and want more keys than most layouts provide to feel comfortable jumping in
   - Depending on your preferences you may realize you dont use them, but having too many keys isn't as inconvenient as too litle keys.
 - People who like to <ins>hover type</ins> or <ins>move their hands around a lot</ins>
   - Some extra keys are better used in this way instead of reaching from the home position.
 - People with <ins>large hands</ins>
   - The stagger and splay were directly based off my own hand's resting position and finger movements. For reference, my hands are [insert measurements]
 - People who are <ins>thumb power users</ins>
   - The thumb cluster layout provides a lot of power, but also may not be great for people with weaker thumbs or smaller hand spans.
